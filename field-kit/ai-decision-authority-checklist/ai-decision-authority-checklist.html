AI Decision Authority Checklist

Use this before AI-assisted QA work becomes release evidence. The goal is to keep facts, suggestions, human judgment, and lifecycle outcomes separate enough that someone can defend the decision later.

Context

Decision chain

Keep each layer separate. Do not let a clean AI suggestion blur into the facts beneath it.

1. System-derived factsLogs, test results, requirements, defects, telemetry, screenshots, source records, or observed behavior.
2. Machine-suggested interpretationWhat AI inferred, grouped, summarized, classified, or proposed.
3. Human-validated judgmentWhat the human owner accepted, rejected, corrected, or left ambiguous.
4. Lifecycle-controlled outcomeWhere the decision landed: defect, test, backlog item, release note, risk record, exception, or gate.

Checklist

Source facts are named

What concrete evidence did the AI output use?

AI suggestion is separated from fact

What did the model infer, summarize, classify, or propose?

Human judgment is explicit

Who accepts, rejects, corrects, or leaves the AI output ambiguous?

Risk or exception is owned

If this creates or accepts risk, who owns it and when is it revisited?

Lifecycle outcome is recorded

Where does this live after the review?

Final authority check

Gut check: if no human can defend the AI-assisted output, it is not decision-grade yet.
